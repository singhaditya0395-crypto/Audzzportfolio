Privacy Policy

What's collected. The only data this site gathers is what you type into the Virtual Coffee questionnaire: name, email, organization, role, discussion topic, and notes, plus your explicit consent to be contacted about it. There are no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or ad trackers running here.

How it's used. Submitting the form emails your answers straight to Aditya's inbox so he can prepare for the conversation. It's used only for that purpose, never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and never used for marketing.

Where it's stored. The email notification is the durable record (subject to your own email provider's retention). A best-effort backup copy is also kept briefly on the server's temporary storage, which can be cleared at any time. Separately, an encrypted copy of your submission (your email address specifically is encrypted at rest) is kept for up to 30 days to power the data-access and deletion requests below, then automatically and permanently deleted.

Access logging. Every time stored submission data is read or deleted, that access is itself logged (timestamp, action, and requester IP) for security auditing.

Outbound links. Links to LinkedIn, YouTube, Substack, and BosonQ Psi take you off this site to services with their own privacy policies.

Your data, your call. Right after you submit the questionnaire, you're shown a personal link that lets you view or permanently delete your stored submission yourself at any time within the 30 days, no need to ask. Lost that link? Email and Aditya will do it for you.
